What We Stand For

Mental well-being should be a fundamental right, not a privilege. We are building a comprehensive support network.

Direct Communication

Facilitating direct communication with qualified mental health professionals for immediate support.

Accessible Care

Advocating for the establishment of free mental health resources accessible to all, regardless of circumstances.

Supportive Community

Building strong networks of support and understanding where healing becomes possible for everyone.

Our Community Programs

We organize impactful semesterly programs designed to foster personal growth and community outreach.

First Semester

  • Webinar Sessions

    Virtual platform for engaging discussions featuring expert speakers on personal development.

  • Community Outreach

    Connecting with the community, encouraging volunteerism, and making a positive impact on local neighborhoods.

Second Semester

  • Self-Discovery Workshops

    Interactive sessions exploring identity, values, and resources for members seeking growth.

  • Radio Program & Summit

    Informative broadcasts and a culminating networking event bringing experts and community together.

Two Ways to Join Us

The Inner Compass Project is open to everyone. You can join as a general member to access resources and community, or apply to be an executive to lead our initiatives.

General Member

Perfect for individuals looking for a supportive mental health community and resources.

  • Access to all community workshops & webinars
  • Peer support network and safe spaces
  • Free mental health resource toolkits
  • Invitations to the Inner Compass Summit

Executive Team

For passionate advocates ready to take on leadership roles and drive international change.

  • Global Recognition: Contributions on an international level
  • Internships: Access to impactful practical experience
  • Leadership Training: Cultivate essential skills
  • Networking: Connect with mentors and professionals
